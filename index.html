Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 2026

Overview

FlexGrabber Pro ("the App") is a tool designed to assist Amazon Flex drivers in automating the offer acceptance process using Android Accessibility Services. We are committed to protecting your privacy.

FlexGrabber Pro does NOT collect, transmit, store, or share any personal data.

Data Collection

All data processed by the App remains entirely on your device. The App does not:

  • Connect to any external servers
  • Collect personal information
  • Track your location
  • Access your contacts, photos, or files
  • Share data with third parties
  • Use analytics or tracking SDKs

How the App Works

The App uses Android's Accessibility Service API to:

  • Read text content displayed in the Amazon Flex app on your device
  • Perform tap actions on buttons within the Amazon Flex app
  • Process filter preferences stored locally on your device

All processing occurs locally on your device. No data leaves your phone.

Permissions

The App requests the following permissions:

  • Accessibility Service: To read and interact with the Amazon Flex app interface
  • System Alert Window: To display the floating control widget
  • Post Notifications: To notify you when the service is running
  • Vibrate: To provide haptic feedback on actions
  • Billing: To manage your subscription through Google Play

Subscription & Payment

FlexGrabber Pro offers a subscription service managed entirely through Google Play Billing:

  • 14-day free trial upon first installation
  • AUD $20/month subscription after trial expires
  • Payment is processed by Google Play. We do not have access to your payment information
  • Subscription status is verified locally on your device

Third-Party Services

The App interacts with the following third-party service:

  • Google Play Billing: For subscription management
  • Amazon Flex App: The App reads and interacts with the Amazon Flex app interface using Accessibility Services

Children's Privacy

The App is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect information from children.
