# Privacy Policy for FlexGrabber Pro **Last Updated:** July 29, 2026 FlexGrabber Pro ("we," "our," or "us") respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how our application handles your data. --- ## 1. AccessibilityService API Disclosure FlexGrabber Pro uses Android's **AccessibilityService API** to provide its core functionality. * **Purpose:** The API is used strictly to perform automated on-screen taps and gestures based on your configured filters, helping reduce physical hand strain and repetitive motion fatigue. * **No Data Collection:** The AccessibilityService API does **NOT** collect, read, store, or transmit any personal data, passwords, sensitive screen content, messages, or keylog inputs. * **Local Processing:** All actions performed by the service take place locally on your device in real time. --- ## 2. Information Collection and Usage FlexGrabber Pro is designed with privacy in mind: * **No Personal Data Collected:** We do not collect, track, or share personal identifying information, location data, contacts, or browsing history. * **Local Settings:** Your station filters, time windows, and pay thresholds are stored locally on your device and are never sent to external servers. --- ## 3. Third-Party Services FlexGrabber Pro does not sell, rent, or share user data with third parties or ad networks. The app uses standard **Google Play Services** strictly to manage app licensing and Google Play Billing for subscriptions. --- ## 4. Permission Control and Data Deletion * **Managing Permissions:** You can enable or disable the Accessibility Service at any time via your device settings (**Settings > Accessibility > Installed Apps > FlexGrabber Pro**). * **Data Deletion:** Because all user preferences are saved locally on your device, clearing app data in your device settings or uninstalling the app permanently removes all saved preferences. --- ## 5.
